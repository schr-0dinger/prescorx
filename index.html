Why Kavum?

Designed around the rhythm of a clinic.

Structured, not stiff

Capture patient details, diagnosis, medicines, advice, and follow-up in a clean format that is ready to export.

History that stays close

Keep patients and prescriptions in one encrypted archive. Search by name, phone, or patient ID and start repeat visits faster.

Consultation-aware

Log consultation mode, consent, and caregiver details when needed, so the record matches the way the visit happened.

Privacy by default

Medical records are protected with AES-256-GCM encryption and doctor-controlled account access.

Workflow

From consultation to shareable prescription in one flow.

  1. 1

    Set up your profile once

    Add your name, registration number, qualification, clinic details, and draw or upload your signature once. It appears on every prescription automatically.

  2. 2

    Document the visit

    Record patient info, diagnosis, and medicines in a structured form. Returning patients are pre-filled from history. Telemedicine fields are available when needed.

  3. 3

    Preview and share

    Review the prescription before sending. Export as a polished PDF, save as an image, or share directly to the patient's WhatsApp in one tap.
